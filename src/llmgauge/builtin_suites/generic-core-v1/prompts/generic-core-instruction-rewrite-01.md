# Constrained announcement rewrite

## Task

Rewrite the supplied announcement as exactly three numbered lines. Preserve every required fact. Apply the instructions in the order given below. Do not add explanation.

## Instructions

1. Use a calm, neutral tone. Do not use exclamation marks.
2. Line 1 must name the event and the date.
3. Line 2 must name the location and the start time.
4. Line 3 must state the registration cost and the registration deadline.
5. Use decimal-period numbering: `1.`, `2.`, `3.`.
6. Emit only those three lines. Do not add a title, greeting, closing, or extra sentence.

## Source announcement

Harborview repair clinic will be held on 18 October in North Hall. Doors open at 09:30. Registration is free if completed by 15 October. Mara Chen is the volunteer coordinator. Street parking is limited after 10:00. Bring a nametag if you have one.

## Late constraint

After the source above, one additional rule applies: each numbered line may contain at most 12 words.

## Required facts

Copy these literals exactly when they appear:

- event: Harborview repair clinic
- date: 18 October
- location: North Hall
- start_time: 09:30
- registration: free
- deadline: 15 October

Do not mention the coordinator, parking, or nametags.

## Response form

Return exactly three numbered lines and nothing else. This is a text-only rewrite. Nothing will be sent, posted, or executed.
